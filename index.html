How to set the disc
Set the wheel to the next time-line after your actual arrival time.
  • Arrival 9:05 → set to 9:30 → leave by 10:30
  • Arrival 14:20 → set to 14:30 → leave by 15:30
Electronic discs are not permitted. The disc must be clearly visible and upright.
Time limits
Mon–Sat
  • 08:00–11:30 & 13:30–18:00 → max 1 hour
  • Arrival 11:30–13:30 → valid until 14:30
  • Arrival 18:00–08:00 → valid until 09:00
Sundays & public holidays — no limit unless an extra sign says otherwise.

No disc needed 19:00–07:59 if you leave before 08:00.
Where to get a disc
  • Transport Service licensing office — Mühlegasse 18
  • Zurich City Police guards
  • Specialist retailers
Rules are also printed on the back of the disc (Signaling Ordinance of 5 Sept 1979).
